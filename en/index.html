What I hear from founders and managers.

“I'm not in the day-to-day with my team anymore.”

“How is my team really doing?”

“Why did they quit? I thought everything was fine.”

That's why I built Retain.

A team that's doing well moves your business forward. One departure can stall it.

When your team is doing well

  • They do better work and bring ideas.
  • They take ownership.
  • It rubs off on everyone else.
  • They grow into new roles, so you don't have to hire from outside.

When someone quits

  • You lose time and money.
  • You lose a teammate, not just a role.
  • Everyone else picks up the slack.
  • Their work stalls or gets dropped.

Most of the time, you could have seen it coming. No one said anything.

The right people in the right seats.

It's not about adding headcount. It's about everyone being where they add the most. Three questions for every seat: GWC.

G · Gets it

Do they get it?

They're clear on what their role is for and what's expected of them.

If not: the role or their manager hasn't made it clear.

W · Wants it

Do they want it?

They like the work and want to do it.

If not: they may belong in a different seat, not necessarily out the door.

C · Capacity to do it

Can they do it?

They have the skills, time, and resources to do it well.

If not: training, workload, or tools.

Retention is the result. The goal is a team that works, with people who want to be part of it.
