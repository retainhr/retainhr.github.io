Hiring well is half the job.

A bad hire costs time, money, and wear on the team. And a good one can still be lost if no one supports them when they join. That's why we don't treat them as two separate things.

What we do.

Your search, end to end

We learn what you need: the role, the team, and the culture. We source, screen, and bring you a few candidates, but the right ones. We stay with you until the person starts.

  • 100% success-based: if you don't hire, you don't pay.
  • 3-month guarantee: if the hire doesn't work out or leaves, we repeat the search.

A new hire's first months

We don't disappear when they sign. We meet with the new hire during their first months: how they're settling in, whether they're clear on what's expected, and how things are with their manager. We tell you what to adjust before it becomes a problem.

Included with the search.

Your team, person by person

We listen to your team through 1:1 check-ins, pulse surveys, and engagement surveys. We bring you how each person is doing, who needs attention, and what to do, and we go over it together.

The right people in the right seats.

It's not about adding headcount. It's about everyone being where they add the most. Three questions for every seat: GWC.

G · Gets it

Do they get it?

They're clear on what their role is for and what's expected of them.

If not: the role or their manager hasn't made it clear.

W · Wants it

Do they want it?

They like the work and want to do it.

If not: they may belong in a different seat, not necessarily out the door.

C · Capacity to do it

Can they do it?

They have the skills, time, and resources to do it well.

If not: training, workload, or tools.

Retention is the result. The goal is a team that works, with people who want to be part of it.
